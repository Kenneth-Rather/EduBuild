The Water Cycle

Water is always moving around our planet. This movement is called the water cycle.

When the sun heats water in oceans, lakes, and rivers, the water turns into a gas called water vapor. This is called evaporation.

As water vapor rises into the sky, it cools down and turns back into tiny drops of water. These drops come together to make clouds. This is called condensation.

When the drops in a cloud get too big and heavy, they fall back to the ground as rain, snow, sleet, or hail. This is called precipitation.

The water collects in oceans, lakes, rivers, and under the ground. This is called collection. Then the sun heats the water again, and the cycle starts over.
